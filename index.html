About

I'm Osian, the founder of Ascent Web Design, based in Swansea, Wales.

Ascent Web Design launched in 2026, building on over two years of self-taught study in web development, design, and digital marketing. What started as a personal interest in coding grew into a business focused on helping small businesses establish a stronger online presence.

Alongside web design, I work in sales, which gives me a practical understanding of how businesses attract customers and build trust. That experience influences every project, from website structure and user experience to SEO and marketing.

Whether it's a simple brochure website or a more tailored solution, my focus is on creating professional websites that look great, perform well, and support real business goals.
