About

I'm Osian Llywelyn, a freelance web designer and developer based in Swansea, Wales, and the person behind Ascent Web Design. I work with small businesses that need a website that does a proper job, not just one that looks good.

Before moving into web design I worked in sales, so I think about what turns a visitor into a customer. That shapes everything I build, from how a page is laid out to how easy it is to get in touch.

I handle the design, the build and the SEO, and can support your marketing too, so you deal with one person rather than several. I'll recommend the right platform for what you need, including WordPress, and I'll explain things in plain English along the way.
